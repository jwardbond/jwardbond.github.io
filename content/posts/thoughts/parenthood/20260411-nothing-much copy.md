I think because we know the ultrasound is coming up, we have put almost everything baby-related on hold. We haven't told anyone anything, we haven't made any more changes to the house. We haven't even really noticed any new changes in Emma.

Well that's not _entirely_ true. Emma has a lot more food aversions now. Things she liked last week (turkey, crackers) seem a little more gaggy. It's driving me a bit batty, because I filled up the freezer with SOOO many foods she liked last week, only for her to turn her nose up at them this week. One constant:

> "bitches love fruit" - Emma.

Ryan and Jessie announced that they were having a baby this week. Emma and I got so jealous that they were able to tell people, while we were stuck in this weird limbo. I thought the jealousy would be over by now T.T I thought I was supposed to put all these silly things behind me now that I am Dad. Alas, having a kid doesn't solve your crippling problems with envy, you have to fix those on your own.

Note to baby: I hope you get your mom's self-confidence. She's still envious, but she doesn't let it control her as much as I do.
