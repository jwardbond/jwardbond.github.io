The [Philippine tarsier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_tarsier) is a member of the approximately 45-million-year-old family Tarsiidae, whose name is derived from its elongated "tarsus" or ankle bone.

It's also the "weird but cute" creature our app used as a size comparison for you last week (that, and a batman action figure).

In the past couple weeks Emma has _barely_ started showing. She says she can feel her uterus a lot more, especially when she lies down or rolls over on the bed. She said it feels "hard", but I don't have a great baseline for this. The other day she thought she felt you flutter in there!

Apparently you have started to develop hearing, so I have been trying to read to you a little more. You are going to come out of there loving 1001 arabian nights. Probably on a flying carpet, or so much more beautiful than the moon that you are immediately whisked away by some conniving ifrit.

I keep having these elysian dreams, right as I drift off to sleep. In them, I am holding you and laughing and smiling and Emma is there watching. These are almost (comically) like the flashbacks of some wounded protagonist in an action movie. Like the memories Russel Crowe has in Gladiator. Except mine are flash-forwards, to happier times to come.

There was a big block sale this past weekend, and I picked you up some clothes. I think these were the first ones I have ever picked out myself! I hope you like them :D.

The only other crazy thing that happened this past week was that a bunch of 12 year-olds were arrested for stealing a car and got into a shootout with the cops down the street from our house. I don't feel too alarmed about this, because our neighbourhood is pretty safe, but I am wondering circumstances have to exist for a bunch of 12 year olds to do something like this. I feel fairly confident that I will be able to prevent them from happening in your life.

Love you!
