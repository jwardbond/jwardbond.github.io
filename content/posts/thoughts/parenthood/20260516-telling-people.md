Morning Pipes,

(I think that is what we are going with. Emma seems to be getting more and more comfortable with the name, even if it is rambunctious.)

It's been a couple weeks, but things change and they don't. You grow. We get more results (and more certainty). More and more people find out. But I still feel pretty much the same person I was yesterday.

A couple of weeks ago I was trying to imagine the person I wanted to be for you. I only had 7 months to learn how to be more kind and hard working and generous and curious and adventerous and all the other things I wanted to impart to you [^1]! The past couple weeks I've started coming to terms with how silly an idea this was. Change is slow, inexorable, and hard. In some senses its even harder when you are making a concious effort to do it. It seems like the odds are high that I come out at the end of this (or you do, rather) only slightly different from the person I was 9 months earlier. The more likely outcome is that you and I grow slowly together, changing eachother in ways neither of us can predict or plan for.

Good ways though, I expect, and I am looking forward to it.

## Telling people

Now that we have the NT results back, we are finally comfortable telling the big wide world about you---although we are still trying to be a little more private than people seem to have been in the past couple decades. Most reactions have either been something along the lines of "I knew/expected that you were" or "Congrats" and then moving on. I'm not exactly upset about this, but it is a little jarring to realize that this thing that is such a huge life event for me is just another day for everyone else. It's like telling people I've won the lottery, except that to me the prize was a billion dollars and to them the prize was five bucks.

The other feeling that I have been having is that there is a limited number of people to tell, but I get such a thrill out of telling them. I don't want it to end!

Here is a list of people I have told, and their reactions.

**Yash**
I told Yash over dinner. His friend Nick was there as well. He widended his eyes slightly and nodded and said congrats, and bought me dinner. I think his lack of surprise was due to the fact that Steven accidentally spilled the beans a couple weeks in advance. No worries though because I spilled Steven's beans. Beans everywhere I guess.

**Dagmawi and Georgia**
I was really excited to tell Dagmawi, but it seemed like he already knew. He'd noticed I'd been going to a lot of doctor's appointments. He was very excited, but has begun giving me all sorts of strange advice like "make sure Emma eats red meat and goat milk". She can barely eat a bowl of cereal, so I don't know how I will make that work. Goat milk and steak popsicles maybe?

Georgia had a more classic reaction, which was nice. She'd asked me a couple hours earlier if Emma and I wanted kids.

**Maaz**
I said "look at this figure I made" and sent him the ultrasound. He said "nice :)" because he didn't actually look at the picture. We kept talking about AI for a while before I pointed out that he never commented on the figure, and then he called and said congrats. We had a nice long chat.

**Alex**
I told alex over messenger. He seemed really excited and invited me out for drinks the next day. I'd assumed we were going to celebrate, but it ended up being a group thing and the focus wasn't really on my impending fatherhood, which I didn't mind.

**My Supervisors**
I was most excited to tell them, and had planned to reveal it at the end of my meeting, but we got stuck talking about fricken graph theory and ended up not having enough time to get to the slide with the ultrasound. What a silly sentence. I met with Elias for a little bit longer after, so I told him, but he really had to go to the bathroom so all he did was tap me on the shoulder and say "congrats" before hurrying off to pee. Then we talked about graph theory for two hours and he said congrats one more time and that was it. I am excited to tell Shoshanna still, but I don't know when I am going to do it.

**Shayan and Ruholla**
I ran into them and their wives when I was biking home, so I told them. They were really happy. And their wives did fist pumps when I said you were a girl, which made me smile.

**Shannon and Jade**
We'd already told them, but this time we got to tell them you are a girl. They all screamed and poor Nora started crying.

## Emma update

Your poor mom has been so sick for the past two weeks. She's been coughing like she has the plague, neither of us have gotten much sleep. I've had to do a few late night pharmacy runs to get drugs that she can take, and she had to get an emergency doctor's appointment to get inhalers. You sure have been bouncing around in there, so if you come out a little funny that's probably why.

Emma is feeling a lot less nauseous these days, and seems to have a little more energy. She has started to complain a little more about back pain, which worries me a little.
