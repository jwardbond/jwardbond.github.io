## Songs

- [Afreen Afreen](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw4tT7SCmaY). This was my introduction to Qawwali music. The Sargam at the end is my favourite part... at least, I think it's called a sargam, and I think its Qawwali music. I have no idea tbh.
- [Sastanàqqàm](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vACZA9dGvV4). Tuareg blues. Had all their music on repeat for a solid few weeks.
- [The Very Day I'm Gone](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BDCxVQCvOTY). I'd never really listened to Nora Brown before, but I got into her after picking up a banjo (except this isn't a banjo song).
